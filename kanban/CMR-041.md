## Description

There is no food source besides "eat raw" on contact - a hunger sink, not a sustainable colony. Add the missing producer side: a Plant designation that wardens with `Skills.plant >= 1` sow on Grass tiles, a slow growth tick, and a Harvest job that converts mature crops into `ItemKind.RawFood` items dropped on the tile (consumed via CMR-036 cook bills).

## Acceptance Criteria

- New `system_farm` in `src/game/Sim/systems/farm.ts` with three stages: `Sown -> Growing -> Mature` driven by a `Crop` bitecs component `{ stage: ui8, growth: ui16 }`. Growth: 1 unit per 60 ticks; matures at `growth >= 600` (~10 minutes of in-game time).
- New `Plant` and `Harvest` worktypes plug into the priority grid (CMR-031).
- Plant designation: paint Grass tiles; warden walks to tile, stands `WORK_TICKS_PLANT=16`, spawns a `Crop` entity at stage Sown.
- Harvest designation auto-fires when a Crop reaches Mature: warden walks, stands `WORK_TICKS_HARVEST=12`, spawns `ItemStack{kind:RawFood,count:1}` and removes the Crop.
- Trampling: a warden walking through a Sown tile has a 1/16 chance (seeded RNG) to destroy the crop - keeps paths off farmland.
- Tests in `tests/sim/farm.test.ts`: (a) full plant->grow->harvest cycle yields one RawFood; (b) trampling destroys a Sown crop deterministically with seed=7; (c) Plant priority=0 means no sowing.
- `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run lint` all green.
